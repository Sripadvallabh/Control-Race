4. Snooker Challenge
--------------------

4.1 Declaring a Challenge
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**1.** On their turn, a player may challenge a color controlled by another
player.

**2.** A snooker challenge cannot be declared immediately after a non-potting
safety shot.

4.2 Resolving the Challenge Shot
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**1.** The challenger plays an extra stroke.

**2.** If the cue ball becomes snookered on the challenged color after that
stroke, and the stroke involved no foul, the owner of the challenged color must
play the next shot.

**3.** If the challenged color is potted during the challenge stroke, it is
spotted before judging whether the cue ball is snookered.

**4.** If the cue ball is not snookered, the initiator loses points equal to
the challenged color's original scoring value, capped at 4 points.

4.3 Owner's Challenge Stroke
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**1.** The forced challenge stroke does not replace the owner's normal turn.
It is an extra stroke only.

**2.** After the challenge stroke is played, the turn continues normally.

**3.** If the challenged player pots the challenged ball, the ball is scored
normally.

**4.** Normal penalties apply on a miss or any other foul.
