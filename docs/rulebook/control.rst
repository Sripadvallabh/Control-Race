Control
-------

Marks
~~~~~

For every color other than red, players keep a tally of marks.

When a player legally pots a color that is not already controlled, that player
adds one mark to that color.

Control Threshold
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

When a player reaches three marks on a color, that color is controlled by that
player.

A controlled color cannot be marked or controlled by any other player.

Each player can control a maximum of three colors.

After a player controls three colors, that player can no longer mark any color.

Controlled Color Scoring
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

When the owner pots one of their controlled colors, they score that color's
normal value.

The owner is guaranteed a minimum score of 4 points when potting one of their
controlled colors. This means yellow and green score 4 for their owner while
controlled.

When a non-owner pots a controlled color, that player receives no points for
the pot. The potted ball is spotted and the non-owner's turn ends immediately.

Control Revoke
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

A player may revoke control of a color only at the end of their turn.

The player may revoke a controlled color only if their other controlled colors
have an equal or greater combined scoring value than the revoked color. For this
calculation, yellow and green each count as 4.

When control is revoked:

* The revoked color becomes uncontrolled.
* The revoking player keeps one mark on that color.
* Other players' frozen marks on that color become active again.
* The revoking player remains liable to snooker challenges on that color until
  it is legally potted as the nominated ball by any player.

A player may have only one active revoke liability.

A player with an active revoke liability cannot revoke another color until the
current liability is cleared.
