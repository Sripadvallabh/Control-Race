3. Control
----------

3.1 Marks
~~~~~~~~~

**1.** For every color other than red, players keep a tally of marks.

**2.** When a player legally pots a color that is not already controlled,
that player adds one mark to that color.

3.2 Control Threshold
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**1.** When a player reaches three marks on a color, that color is controlled
by that player.

**2.** A controlled color cannot be marked or controlled by any other player.

**3.** Each player can control a maximum of three colors.

**4.** After a player controls three colors, that player can no longer mark
any color.

3.3 Controlled Color Scoring
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**1.** When the owner pots one of their controlled colors, they score that
color's normal value.

**2.** The owner is guaranteed a minimum score of 4 points when potting one
of their controlled colors. This means yellow and green score 4 for their owner
while controlled.

**3.** When a non-owner pots a controlled color, that player receives no
points for the pot. The potted ball is spotted and the non-owner's turn ends
immediately.

3.4 Control Revoke
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**1.** A player may revoke control of a color only at the end of their turn.

**2.** The player may revoke a controlled color only if their other
controlled colors have an equal or greater combined scoring value than the
revoked color. For this calculation, yellow and green each count as 4.

**3.** When control is revoked:

* **(a)** The revoked color becomes uncontrolled.
* **(b)** The revoking player keeps one mark on that color.
* **(c)** Other players' frozen marks on that color become active again.
* **(d)** The revoking player remains liable to snooker challenges on that
  color until it is legally potted as the nominated ball by any player.

**4.** A player may have only one active revoke liability.

**5.** A player with an active revoke liability cannot revoke another color
until the current liability is cleared.
