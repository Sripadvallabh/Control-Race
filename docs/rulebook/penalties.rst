Penalties
---------

Penalty points are subtracted from the player's score, to a minimum score of
zero.

If multiple penalties apply to the same shot or situation, only the highest
penalty applies.

The following penalties apply:

* Normal snooker penalties.
* A 30 point penalty for any intentional foul or illegal shot made to manipulate
  score, avoid exceeding the win score, reset position, or create an easier
  route to the win score.

Intentional fouls and illegal score-manipulation shots are arbitrated by
majority vote of the other players.
