Bookkeeping
-----------

Score and Mark Board
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Each player's score and marks are managed by the next player clockwise.

After every legal pot or penalty, the assigned scorer updates the score and
mark board before the next shot.

Any scoring or marking correction must be raised before the next shot is
played.

Once the next shot is played, the board state stands.

Spotting Responsibility
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

By default, balls are spotted by the non-shooting player who is not next to
play.

Players may agree to a different spotting arrangement.

The incoming player must check the table before playing. Once they play, the
table position stands.
