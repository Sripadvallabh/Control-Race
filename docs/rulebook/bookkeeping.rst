7. Bookkeeping
--------------

7.1 Score and Mark Board
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**7.1.1** Each player's score and marks are managed by the next player
clockwise.

**7.1.2** After every legal pot or penalty, the assigned scorer updates the
score and mark board before the next shot.

**7.1.3** Any scoring or marking correction must be raised before the next shot
is played.

**7.1.4** Once the next shot is played, the board state stands.

7.2 Spotting Responsibility
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**7.2.1** By default, balls are spotted by the non-shooting player who is not
next to play.

**7.2.2** Players may agree to a different spotting arrangement.

**7.2.3** The incoming player must check the table before playing. Once they
play, the table position stands.
