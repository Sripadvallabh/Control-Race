1. Setup and Objective
----------------------

1.1 Setup
~~~~~~~~~

**1.1.1** Place the six colors on their respective spots.

**1.1.2** Place a single red ball between the blue and pink spots.

**1.1.3** The game starts with cue ball in hand inside the D.

1.2 Objective
~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**1.2.1** The first player to reach exactly 75 points wins.

**1.2.2** On a turn, a player may nominate any color to pot, except a color
whose scoring value would take that player above the win score.

**1.2.3** All potted balls are spotted after being potted unless a penalty or
challenge rule says otherwise.
