Setup and Objective
-------------------

Setup
~~~~~

Place the six colors on their respective spots.

Place a single red ball between the blue and pink spots.

The game starts with cue ball in hand inside the D.

Objective
~~~~~~~~~

The first player to reach exactly 75 points wins.

On a turn, a player may nominate any color to pot, except a color whose scoring
value would take that player above the win score.

All potted balls are spotted after being potted unless a penalty or challenge
rule says otherwise.
